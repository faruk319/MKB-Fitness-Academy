Our Story

A Legacy of Health & Wellness

Founded in 2019 and proudly located at Sector 29, Ravet, Pune, MKB Fitness Academy has evolved into a comprehensive wellness destination. We serve kids (3+ years), adults, seniors, IT professionals, and athletes.

With a passionate team of trainers and state-of-the-art facilities, we've established ourselves as the top-rated fitness center in the area. We are incredibly proud of our 4.9★ rating on Justdial, supported by over 43+ glowing reviews from our community.
