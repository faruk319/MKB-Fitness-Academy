What is IASTM therapy

Instrument assisted soft tissue mobilisation

IASTM is a specialised hands-on therapy that uses specially designed instruments to detect and treat soft tissue restrictions, scar tissue and fascial adhesions. It helps restore normal movement, reduce pain and improve function.

How it works

  1. Identifies restrictionsFinds areas of scar tissue, adhesions and muscle restriction.
  2. Applies controlled pressureUses specialised instruments to apply targeted pressure.
  3. Breaks down tissueBreaks down abnormal tissue and stimulates natural healing.
  4. Restores mobilityRestores normal tissue mobility, reducing pain and improving function.

Common conditions treated

  • Muscle strains & tension
  • Sports injuries
  • Fascial restrictions
  • Chronic pain
  • Scar tissue & adhesions
  • Post-surgical rehab

  • Repetitive strain injuries
