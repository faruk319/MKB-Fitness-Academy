FAQ

Questions

What are the timings?

6:00 AM – 12:00 PM and 4:00 PM – 10:00 PM, seven days a week. Gym and pool are both open in these hours.

Can I visit before joining?

Yes. Walk in during working hours for a free tour, or message us to book a trial.

Which age groups can swim?

Kids from age 3 and adults of any level. Ladies-only slots with female coaches are available.

Do you have personal trainers?

Yes, certified male and female trainers. Charges are separate from gym membership.

Is parking available?

Yes, for two- and four-wheelers. We're close to Akurdi, Pradhikaran, Nigdi and Kiwale.
